🧘 Amused Mastery & Frame Control

The Golden Rule

She throws a shit test to see if you'll crumble. Amused mastery means you treat every test as a joke. You're the game master. Frame = reality you choose.

Frame Control 101

Never accept her frame. If she says "you're cheap", your frame: "I'm selectively generous." The master never defends, he redefines.

5 Core Response Archetypes

Amplify | Agree & exaggerate | Misdirect with absurdity | Label the test | Reframe to "us"

Why Bad Replies Fail

Defensiveness = guilt. Over-explaining = weakness. Apologizing = loss of frame. Amused Mastery laughs at the test itself.

The Equal-Adult Frame

In Bill Traps, Love Bombs & Chore Wars, the strongest frame is "equal adult, not subordinate." Generous by choice, never by obligation. Warm, never swept up. You take full ownership of your share — because owning it permanently ends the fight. Dominance through competence beats dominance through refusal.
